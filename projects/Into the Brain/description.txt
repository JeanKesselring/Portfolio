From the project pages, Into the Brain is your 2020 bachelor project in Knowledge Visualization / Scientific Visualization at ZHdK. It presents the human brain across multiple scales, guiding viewers from a macroscopic view down to microscopic neural structures in an interactive web experience. The concept grew out of your internship with the Blue Brain Project, with the goal of making complex neurobiological structures accessible to a broader audience.
