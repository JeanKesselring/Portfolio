Into the
Brain

Scientific Viz · Web · ZHdK · 2020
Explore ↗

Into the Brain is my 2020 bachelor project in Knowledge Visualization / Scientific Visualization at ZHdK. It presents the human brain across multiple scales, guiding viewers from a macroscopic view down to microscopic neural structures in an interactive web experience.

The concept grew out of my internship with the Blue Brain Project, with the goal of making complex neurobiological structures accessible to a broader audience.

03

Zauberpark
Installation

Interactive · Sound · Projection Mapping · 2023

An immersive interactive installation developed for the 2023 Zauberpark festival in collaboration with David Wollschlegel. Set outdoors in the freezing December cold, the project combined projection, sensing, and sound to transform the park into a responsive audiovisual space.

Up to four visitors could engage with the installation simultaneously, generating animations and soundscapes through their movement and position.

Omakase

Card Game Design · Illustration · 3D Animation
Visit ↗

Omakase is a sushi-themed card game I designed and developed as an original game project. Players collect valuable sushi combinations, use action cards to disrupt opponents, and compete to build the highest-scoring hand.

The project involved game design, rule balancing, theme development, and creating a playful visual identity around Japanese food culture.

05

mindblob

Learning Platform · Knowledge Graphs · AI · Product Design

A learning environment built on a knowledge graph — so you always see where a topic sits, what it builds on, and what to practise next.

mindblob turns course material — lecture slides, recordings and web sources — into a structured graph of connected concepts. From that graph it generates learning resources such as summaries, quizzes, flashcards, audio and short videos, and tracks mastery concept by concept.

Learners can explore topics spatially, nested inside one another, and practise across connected skills. Educators get a teaching view to shape courses, inspect the learner experience and improve what needs attention.

  • GraphCourses as maps of connected concepts
  • PracticeMastery tracked per concept and skill
  • FormatsSummaries, quizzes, flashcards, audio, video
  • TeachingCreate, review and improve courses

One example course takes learners from kana through JLPT N1 Japanese across more than 10,000 concepts. mindblob grew out of CommonSage, my earlier AI study tool for university courses.
